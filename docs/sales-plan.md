# Velmora: a six-week plan to stronger Black Friday sales

**Decision:** improve mobile buying confidence and prove contribution before increasing paid spend. Evidence is the supplied April–September 2026 analytics. These are proposed tests, not forecasts or causal claims.

| Priority | Change and evidence | Measurement and decision |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Repair mobile checkout** | Instagram mobile had **16,027 sessions, 56 orders and 0.35% conversion**, versus **1.19%** on Instagram desktop. Just **56 of 478** mobile checkouts completed (**11.7%**). Put fabric, fit, making time, shipping and returns near the buy button; preserve the bag. First audit payment failures, unexpected costs and checkout usability. | Instrument product view, size choice, add to bag, checkout, purchase and checkout errors by channel/device. Target **0.50%** Instagram mobile conversion and **15%** checkout completion. Compare equal periods; show uncertainty because orders are sparse. Guard against slower pages, returns and weaker contribution/order. |
| **2. Make occasion and timing decisions clearer** | Add occasion journeys and a catalogue-backed stylist. Match actual occasion, size and budget; show the latest estimated arrival and a no-match state. Email had **52 orders / 1,744 sessions = 2.98% conversion**; this association does not prove incremental lift. | Track helper open, submit, no-match, result click and purchase. Where volume permits, use a randomised holdout; helper users self-select. Target a **15% relative add-to-bag lift** with no rise in late deliveries. Obtain real measurements, care, composition and studio availability first. |
| **3. Gate paid-social scale on contribution** | Paid social: **£3,376 revenue / £2,700 spend = 1.25× ROAS**; **10 orders / 4,198 sessions = 0.24% conversion**. At 55% gross margin, contribution before other costs is **£1,856.80**, or **−£843.20 after ads**. Gross-margin-only break-even ROAS is **1.82×**; fees, shipping and returns raise it. | Pause scaling. Reconcile attribution and run only a capped, occasion-led test after mobile fixes. Review contribution after ads, fees, shipping and returns weekly; do not scale below true break-even. Cap made-to-order commitments at **20 pieces/week**, including current backlog. |

**Black Friday:** use the requested **20% off eight selected pieces**, show original and sale prices together, verify margins and capacity, and stop date-sensitive promotion when the latest estimate misses the occasion. At unchanged costs, a 20% discount reduces gross profit from 55% to 35% of original price (**43.75% of discounted revenue**) before acquisition and fulfilment. Do not invent scarcity, reviews or guaranteed delivery.

**Data caveat:** email desktop recorded 39 checkouts but 38 add-to-carts. Check event definitions and attribution before treating the funnel as sequential. No significance, causal lift or spare capacity is assumed.
