# Final storefront design

The home page opens as a full-screen, dark fashion campaign: a Blender gown in Three.js, oversized editorial type and a high-contrast 20% Black Friday mark. A three-chapter scroll story shifts the garment's position, rotation, camera distance and satin-to-crepe finish. Chapter controls jump between form, fabric and the sale; an accessible hotspot opens a short garment note. Reduced-motion preferences pause ambient movement, and a visible pause control remains available.

J. Stimler informed the image-first full-bleed composition, sparse top navigation, large identity and vivid accent. Palace informed direct category navigation and an image-forward catalogue. These are layout references only: Velmora's name, product copy, artwork, models and graphics are original. Cartier Roadster returned an access-denied response in the reference capture, so no exact Roadster interaction is claimed or copied.

The sale collection, product pages, filters, fit/care/delivery disclosures, bag, and occasion helper retain the supplied catalogue as their source of product facts. The user-selected offer is exactly 20% off eight named products; original prices remain visible. The sale is an assessment concept, without invented urgency, inventory or delivery promises.

## Motion and interaction

- Three original Blender GLB studies can be changed in the garment studio; Three.js supports rotation, material changes, zoom, reset and pause.
- Home-story scroll progress updates chapter text, navigation state, progress, camera framing, gown rotation and illustrative material colour.
- A hotspot changes its label and detail copy with the active story chapter.
- Motion respects `prefers-reduced-motion`; static garment art remains when WebGL cannot initialize.
- REA browser captures checked desktop/mobile home, all three chapter links, mobile navigation to sale, the eight-piece collection, and the garment studio. No page errors appeared in those captures.

The digital garments are concept studies, not product photography, fit previews, sewing patterns or exact scans of catalogue pieces. Presentation materials and the sales plan state this limitation explicitly.
